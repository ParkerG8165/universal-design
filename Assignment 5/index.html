What is a fourteener?

A fourteener is a mountain summit above 14,000 feet. Colorado's peaks vary widely in distance, elevation gain, and technical difficulty. Comparing these factors helps hikers understand what a route involves.

landscape Preparation matters: plan for the return journey, not just reaching the summit.

Before you go

  • Check the mountain weather forecast.
  • Bring water, food, warm layers, and navigation tools.
  • Start early and set a turnaround time.
  • Choose a route that matches your experience and conditions.

For exposed or technical routes, learn about the terrain and appropriate safety precautions before attempting them.
